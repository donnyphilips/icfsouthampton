Pastor’s Welcome.

It is our joy to welcome you to a church that began in March 2025 with a simple but powerful desire—to see lives transformed by the love of Jesus Christ. From the very beginning, God has brought together a beautiful, multicultural congregation, united in worship and faith.

Our vision is to reach the lost with the Gospel of Lord Jesus Christ and to be a light in our community. We believe this is a time for the Church to rise, to be equipped, and to be ready for the great end-time harvest that God has prepared. As a church, we are committed to teaching the Word of God, building strong disciples, and creating a place where everyone can encounter His presence.

Whether you are exploring faith, new to the area, or looking for a church family, you are warmly invited to join us. We pray that you will find a home here, grow in your walk with God, and be empowered to live out His purpose for your life.

May the Lord bless you richly.

Pastor Donny Philip.

Our Calling

Our Mission

Our mission is to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ with clarity, compassion, and the power of the Holy Spirit.

We are committed to creating an environment where individuals and families can encounter God, experience His grace, and grow into mature disciples through biblical teaching, prayer, worship, and fellowship.

At the heart of our mission is the call to seek and save the lost, sharing the message of salvation throughout Southampton and beyond.

Our Hope

Our Vision

Our vision is to be a Christ-centred, Spirit-filled, soul-winning church, passionately proclaiming the Gospel and seeing lives transformed through Jesus Christ. We pray for a fresh move of the Holy Spirit, bringing spiritual renewal, healing, and the manifestation of God’s power. Our desire is to raise believers who will be faithful witnesses for Christ across Southampton, Hampshire, the UK, and the nations.

Our Reach

Equipping Leaders & Reaching Nations

We are passionate about supporting pastors, church leaders, and ministries across different nations.

Through discipleship, training, prayer, and partnership, we seek to strengthen local churches and equip leaders to effectively share the Gospel.

Our desire is to see healthy, Spirit-filled churches established around the world and to work together in fulfilling Christ's command to seek and save the lost.

Our Family

Our Church Family

Immanuel Christian Fellowship is a welcoming Pentecostal church family where people of all ages and backgrounds can belong, grow, and serve together.

Whether you are exploring faith, new to Southampton, or looking for a church home, we invite you to join us as we worship God, study His Word, and build meaningful relationships centred on Christ.

Ministry

Children's Ministry

At Immanuel Christian Fellowship, we are passionate about helping children and young people grow in their relationship with Jesus Christ. We believe they are not only the future of the Church, but an important part of God's Kingdom today.

Through our Youth Meetings, children and young people are encouraged to discover and develop their God-given gifts. They have opportunities to share God's Word, lead worship, perform skits and dramas, participate in Bible quizzes, and take part in special programmes for different age groups.

Our weekly Sunday School provides age-appropriate biblical teaching using a nationally recognised curriculum. Through Bible lessons, Scripture memorisation, worship, prayer and interactive learning, we seek to build a strong foundation in God's Word.

Bible Learning • Worship & Songs • Prayer

Sacrament

Lord's Supper

At Immanuel Christian Fellowship, we observe Holy Communion once a month, remembering the sacrifice of Jesus Christ on the Cross of Calvary for the forgiveness of our sins.

As we share the bread and the cup, we remember His death and resurrection and proclaim our hope in His return for His Church.

It is also a time for self-examination, repentance, renewal and forgiveness, as we extend to others the forgiveness we have received through Christ.

What We Believe

Statement of Faith

These foundational truths guide our worship, discipleship, ministry and mission as a Pentecostal church in Southampton.

1. The Bible

We believe the Bible is the verbally inspired Word of God and the final authority for faith and life.

2 Timothy 3:16 • 2 Peter 1:21

2. One True God

We believe in one God eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

Deuteronomy 6:4 • 1 Kings 8:60

3. Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God who was born of the Virgin Mary, crucified, buried, resurrected and now intercedes for believers.

John 3:16 • Romans 5:9-10

4. Repentance

We believe all people have sinned and must repent to receive forgiveness and reconciliation with God.

Romans 3:23 • Luke 24:47

5. Salvation

We believe justification, regeneration and the new birth come through faith in Jesus Christ.

Romans 3:24 • Romans 5:1

6. Sanctification

We believe sanctification follows salvation through the blood of Christ, God's Word and the work of the Holy Spirit.

1 Thessalonians 5:23 • John 17:17

7. Holiness

We believe holiness is God's standard for every believer.

1 Thessalonians 4:3 • Hebrews 12:14

8. Baptism in the Holy Spirit

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit as an empowering experience for Christian living and service.

Acts 1:8 • Ephesians 5:18

9. Speaking in Tongues

We believe speaking in tongues is the initial evidence of the baptism in the Holy Spirit.

Acts 2:1-4 • Acts 10:44-46

10. Divine Healing

We believe divine healing is provided through Christ's atoning work.

Isaiah 53:4-5 • Matthew 8:16-17

11. Water Baptism

We believe believers should be baptized by immersion in the name of the Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

Matthew 28:19 • Mark 16:16

12. Lord's Supper

We believe in observing the Lord's Supper and the washing of the saints' feet.

Luke 22:19-20 • John 13:4-17

13. Second Coming

We believe in the premillennial return of Jesus Christ and His thousand-year reign.

1 Thessalonians 4:13-18 • Acts 1:9-11

14. Resurrection & Eternal Judgment

We believe in the bodily resurrection of all people, eternal life for the righteous and eternal punishment for the wicked.

Philippians 3:20-21 • John 11:25-26
